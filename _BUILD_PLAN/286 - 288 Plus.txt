Here is the complete **Billing & Monetization Architecture** for the entire platform. 
The system uses a **Hybrid Multi-Stream Model**:
1. **SaaS Subscription Tiers** (Base access & capacity)
2. **Unified Compute & AI Metering** (1 Credit = $0.01 consumption unit)
3. **Marketplace & Transactional Take-Rates** (Downstream revenue cuts & developer royalties)
This creates the **Self-Funding Business Loop**—where an autonomous venture uses its own customer revenue to pay for its edge compute and AI tokens, with remaining profits paid out to the owner.

# 6. How It Is Enforced in the Codebase
### 1. Pre-Run Gatekeeper Check (`workers/agents/src/do/NaelaoAgentDO.ts`)
Before an agent executes an expensive tool turn (container build or paid LLM inference), `enforceRunGates()` validates the balance:
```typescript
// Pre-flight check before spending compute:
const isFunded = await checkProjectWallet(spaceId, tenantId);
if (!isFunded.allowed) {
  return Response.json({
    error_code: "insufficient_credits",
    message: "This business wallet has depleted its credits. Top up or let customer sales fund it."
  }, { status: 402 });
}
```
### 2. Post-Run Atomic Debit (`workers/agents/src/do/NaelaoAgentDO.ts`)
When the run finishes:
```typescript
// Settle real LLM tokens and Cloudflare compute duration
await settleRunBilling({
  spaceId,
  tokensUsed,
  inputTokens,
  outputTokens,
  tier: executedTier,
  durationMs: executionDuration
});
```
### 3. Automated Payout & Reseller Rollup (`workers/billing/src/lemon-squeezy.ts`)
When a template sale or storefront checkout succeeds:
```typescript
// Record developer/partner cut in reseller_ledger for monthly payout
await recordBuilderRevenueShare(db, share, event, builderFeeCents, providerEventId, false);
```
