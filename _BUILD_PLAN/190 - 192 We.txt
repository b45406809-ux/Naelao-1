We said user gets their own space/business assistant that they connect their own channels like WhatsApp or telegram etc should this also be there in non autonomous business templates the cowork templates
**YES, absolutely.** In fact, having WhatsApp and Telegram connected to non-autonomous / vertical cowork spaces is **one of your biggest competitive advantages over Harvey AI, Legora, and Claude Cowork.**
Products like Harvey AI or desktop Claude are **trapped on a desktop browser**. A lawyer walking into a courtroom, a doctor doing hospital rounds, an architect on a construction site, or an investment banker in a taxi cannot easily open a 15-inch laptop to check a document.
Giving every professional a **personal WhatsApp / Telegram channel to their firm's Cowork Space** turns the system into an on-the-go **Executive Chief of Staff**.
---
### 1. The 3 Killer Use Cases for Channels in Cowork Mode
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    COWORK MODE: THE MOBILE FIRM COPILOT                     │
│                                                                             │
│ [ Professional's Phone (WhatsApp / Telegram) ]                              │
│                 │                                                           │
│                 ├──► 1. Instant Document & Case RAG (Query on-the-go)       │
│                 ├──► 2. Voice-to-Workspace Ingestion (Audio / PDF uploads)  │
│                 └──► 3. One-Tap HITL Approvals (Review drafts & alerts)     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
#### A. Instant Document & Case RAG (On-the-Go Querying)
* **Lawyer in a courtroom:** Texts Telegram:  
  > *"@LexPulse, what was the exact indemnification liability cap we agreed to in Section 4.2 of the Acme draft?"*  
  * The Space Assistant searches the project's Vectorize RAG/SQLite virtual filesystem and replies on Telegram in 2 seconds with the exact clause and citation.
* **PE Associate in a board meeting:**  
  > *"What was the EBITDA margin adjustment for Company X in the Q3 memo?"*  
  * The Assistant queries the financial project model and returns the exact figure.
#### B. Mobile Document & Audio Ingestion (Voice Memos & PDFs)
* **Doctor on hospital rounds:** Sends a 30-second WhatsApp voice note describing patient symptoms. The Assistant transcribes it, formats it into a standard SOAP note, and saves it into the patient's project workspace.
* **Architect on a job site:** Snaps a photo of a foundation beam on WhatsApp. The Assistant checks the photo notes against the project's municipal permit requirements.
* **Consultant forwarding a deck:** Forwards a 20-page PDF pitch deck via Telegram. The Assistant ingests it, extracts the financials, and updates the deal project journal.
#### C. Instant One-Tap HITL Approvals (Human-in-the-Loop)
Instead of forcing the professional to sit at a desktop to approve work:
* When a paralegal subagent finishes drafting an NDA or an SEC filing, the partner gets a WhatsApp alert:
  > *"I completed the initial redline draft for the Vanguard NDA. Detected 1 risky indemnity clause. [Approve Draft] or reply with edits."*
* The partner replies: *"Change liability to mutual, then proceed"*, and the agent applies the AST patch immediately.

The same scan-to-connect QR code and channel pairing infrastructure (`workers/agents/src/launchpad/channel-pairing.ts`) works in both modes, but with different audiences:
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🚀 AUTONOMOUS VENTURE MODE                                   │ 💼 VERTICAL COWORK / COPILOT MODE                           │

│ • **Audience:** Outside Public Customers.                   │ • **Audience:** Internal Firm Professionals & Partners.     │
│ • **Role:** 24/7 AI Sales Receptionist & Support Desk.      │ • **Role:** Private Mobile Executive Copilot.               │
│ • **Example:** Customers text WhatsApp to book a haircut    │ • **Example:** Lawyer texts Telegram to query case law      │
│   or ask for pricing.                                       │   and review contract clauses.                              │
│ • **Access Control:** Restricted via `AgentFirewall` to     │ • **Access Control:** Authenticated to the verified         │
│   Tier 1 / RAG tools (cannot access code or files).         │   professional's project permissions.                       │
│ • **Proactive Triggers:** Nightly revenue recaps & alerts.  │ • **Proactive Triggers:** Filing deadlines & urgent alerts. │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3. How to Configure This in the Blueprint (`blueprint.json`)
In your declarative blueprint, you simply specify what role the channel plays:
```typescript
export interface BusinessBlueprint {
  id: "tpl_legal_cowork_lexpulse";
  title: "LexPulse Legal Cowork Studio";
  template_mode: "vertical_cowork";
  // Channel Configuration
  channel_config: {
    // In Cowork mode, the channel acts as the Private Partner Copilot
    channel_role: "PRIVATE_EXECUTIVE_COPILOT"; // vs "PUBLIC_CUSTOMER_BOT"
    allowedChannels: ["telegram", "whatsapp", "slack"];
    enableVoiceMemoTranscription: true;
    enableDocumentIngestion: true;
    notifyOnPendingApprovals: true; // Pings Telegram when a draft is ready for review
  };
  // Pre-configured projects & knowledge seeds...
}
```

### Summary
**Keep channel connectivity for ALL templates.** 
* In **Autonomous Venture templates**, WhatsApp/Telegram is how **outside customers** talk to the business.
* In **Vertical Cowork templates**, WhatsApp/Telegram is how **busy professionals** direct their AI firm and access their files from anywhere in the world.
