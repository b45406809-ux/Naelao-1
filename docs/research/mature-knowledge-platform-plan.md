# Mature knowledge platform implementation plan

## 1. Mission, baseline and constraints

Bring Naelao's knowledge experience to the level expected of a mature, multi-tenant business knowledge platform: diverse data ingestion, accurate permission-aware retrieval, continuous freshness, citations, guided no-code administration, extension/assistant integration, evaluation, recovery and predictable cost. Build on the current Knowledge Worker and host authorization; use capable managed services or open-source components behind replaceable adapters where they meet the requirements. When Cloudflare does not provide a required feature or its contract is unsuitable, implement the missing control in Naelao or use a suitably isolated, licensed, approved alternative; do not assume a particular provider or paid product is universally available.

This is an implementation plan, not a claim that Naelao already meets these goals or that any provider works in a given deployment. Source observations below were checked against `Mcbt5s-Backup-2` at `af68ee60e6a439eec67188b3ef92dafc176a2546` on 15 November 2026; the active implementation branch may be newer. Before coding, reconcile the latest source, requirements and progress. Integrate with `platform-completion-and-guided-templates-plan.md` sections 5, 9.5, 9.7, 10 and 11, the managed-services and unified workspace plans, and existing P05/P22/knowledge records; preserve newer work and do not replace an active plan or reset the worktree.

Keep existing tenant, Space, Project, user, role, extension, customer-channel and jurisdiction boundaries, authorization, deletion/erasure, billing and provider contracts. Canonical document identity, grants, policy, source lifecycle and truthful publication status belong to Naelao. Provider indexes, caches, mirrors, summaries and extracted content are derived data and must not independently create access or override tombstones. Keep rollout flags off until the relevant gates pass; do not silently relax access or switch providers.

## 2. Product model and collection authority

- Offer named collections and sources with immutable tenant/Space/Project and optional installation/user scope, owner, audience, document versions, status, labels, retention, language, data classification, policy revision and provenance. Support Space-wide, project-restricted, user-restricted and explicitly published external subsets without exposing private project existence. Define whether a principal has discover, read, use-for-answer, manage, share, delete or administer rights separately.
- A collection is not automatically public because an extension can use it. Space-shared channel extensions start with no Project Knowledge unless an authorized owner/admin grants an explicit published selection, subject to existing signed manifests and exact installation/provider scope. Recheck current source, membership, role, grant, policy and installation before each retrieval, citation and effect. Revocation invalidates relevant cached results and stops future reads; avoid leaking source identifiers through search counts or errors.
- Keep ordinary conversation attachments scoped to their original conversation unless explicitly added to an authorized collection with preview and consent. Preserve source identity and do not treat a citation or coordination fact as permission to read the original.
- Provide create/edit/version/publish/unpublish/archive/delete, ownership transfer, export and restore through host-authorized versioned APIs. Make configuration, access and content changes auditable with minimized sensitive data. Define lifecycle behavior for disabled extensions, leaving users, deleted Spaces/projects, publisher changes, legal holds and retained backups.

## 3. Ingestion, parsing and document management

- Reconcile and fix the UI/backend size and scope mismatch first. Present exact capability/size limits, file type, scope, status, parser and retry guidance before submission. Support paste, text/markdown/code/CSV and PDF as verified; use streaming/bounded upload where safe, and support resumable/idempotent jobs without duplicate indexes or bills. Limits are configuration and provider dependent, not a promise of arbitrary document size.
- Add capability-gated DOCX, spreadsheet, presentation, HTML, image and scanned PDF ingestion where approved parsers support them. Extract structure, headings, page/section and table provenance; preserve numeric values, units, column relationships and source coordinates. Show side-by-side or equivalent preview with extraction warnings and confidence where meaningful; allow correction and approval before publishing. Do not assume OCR, tables, handwriting or low-quality scans are always accurate. Reject unsafe, unsupported or password-protected files with actionable status.
- Research and benchmark Cloudflare's [AI Search data sources and OCR](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-search/configuration/data-source/) against existing Naelao parsers and alternatives such as [Docling](https://github.com/docling-project/docling) and [RAGFlow](https://github.com/infiniflow/ragflow). Check each dependency and model's license, runtime, security, resource cost, jurisdiction, egress, retention and version. If an external/container parser is chosen, run it as an explicitly admitted scoped service with bounded CPU, memory, time, file size and archive recursion; isolate untrusted files, block unauthorized network access and prevent malware/zip-bomb/path traversal/SSRF. Never expose provider credentials to source documents or extensions.
- Track source, original content hash, extraction version, parsed artifact hash, parser/model version, chunk version and lineage. Make ingest steps durable and idempotent; prevent retries, concurrent deletion, changed content and lost responses from publishing outdated or partial indexes. Track pending, processing, needs-review, indexed, failed, deleting, deleted and reindex-required states truthfully. Preserve the existing small-document and workflow paths until migration and parity are proven.

## 4. Source connectors and freshness

- Implement a source adapter contract for manual upload, approved websites and approved provider connectors. Start with explicit manual refresh; add scheduled/incremental sync per supported source. Store source connection reference and scoped grants in the host, never in extension code. Recheck connector permission on retrieval where necessary and define freshness/availability when provider access cannot be verified.
- Sync via bounded pages, cursors, generation/fences, idempotency and durable checkpoints. Capture version/ETag/change tokens where reliable; avoid unnecessary re-embedding. Model retries, rate limits, pagination, webhook duplication, out-of-order updates, deletions, lost responses, tombstones, stale cursors and full rescan recovery. Show last attempt/success, next run, changed/failed/skipped counts, scope, staleness and estimated/actual costs to authorized administrators.
- Website ingestion honors configured origin/path, robots and applicable provider/site terms; apply existing SSRF protections to every redirect and resolved destination, bound crawl depth, bytes, file types, time and concurrency, and treat page content as untrusted. Source access tokens and signed URLs must not leak into document text, results, logs or model prompts.
- For third-party access-control sync, define source-to-Naelao identity mapping and intersection rules; fail closed when freshness or authorization cannot be established. Do not assume a provider's file access grants map automatically to Naelao roles or that deleting a connector deletes provider-owned originals.

## 5. Retrieval, citations and quality

- Implement independent lexical and dense candidate generation, each constrained by enforceable tenant/Space/Project/user/role/collection and deletion policy before results can influence ranking or be returned. Then deduplicate and fuse authorized candidates, for example via reciprocal rank fusion. A candidate lexical backend may be Cloudflare AI Search or another supported scoped index; evaluate D1/SQLite full-text capability and operational limits before choosing it, rather than assuming FTS is supported or cheap. Keep current candidate-BM25 path as the compatibility baseline until a secure replacement is tested.
- Support exact identifiers, typo tolerance where appropriate, filters, hybrid/vector/keyword modes, per-collection policy, bounded top-k and optional reranking. Reranking models, query rewriting and generation are separate capability/cost choices; preserve original query and source provenance. Apply calibrated no-answer thresholds only after evaluation; show uncertainty, stale/conflicting sources and abstain when evidence is absent. Never rely on a model or relevance score to authorize access.
- Use bounded, structure-aware, configurable chunking, stable identifiers and source coordinates; evaluate different approaches for tables, code, policies, long documents and short records. Review the currently different synchronous and large-workflow chunking paths and embedding token limits. Reindex deterministically on parser/chunk/model changes; do not assume a single chunk size or model is optimal. Keep authorized original source and accessible citations, including page/section/version where available; check current access and content hash when opening citations and avoid logging raw private content.
- Evaluate English and multilingual embedding/reranking models on representative Zimbabwean English, Shona and Ndebele queries, cross-language searches, medicine names and product codes. Do not claim language support without measured evidence and appropriate native-speaker review. Version models and vector dimensions, run a shadow/dual-index migration with quota and deletion parity, compare quality and cost, and support rollback without exposing stale or revoked documents.
- Test optional query-time web grounding separately from customer-controlled knowledge. Clearly label web results, source provenance and freshness; they do not become approved business policy or enter a collection unless explicitly authorized. Treat retrieved documents, OCR and web content as untrusted and never let them override host policy or authorize actions.

## 6. Cloudflare AI Search and fallback evaluation

Cloudflare's newer [AI Search filtering](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-search/configuration/retrieval/filtering/) documents pre-retrieval metadata filtering; [hybrid search](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-search/configuration/indexing/hybrid-search/) documents independent vector and keyword search and optional reranking; [multitenancy](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-search/how-to/multitenancy/) documents per-tenant instances and shared-index filtering. This is different from the older AutoRAG route Naelao currently disables. Revalidate the current API and terms before use.

- Implement a disabled-by-default, replaceable AI Search evaluation adapter with exact source/tenant/Space/Project/collection mapping. Compare per-tenant/per-scope instances and shared indexes; neither automatically solves user/role/extension ACL. Validate filter expressiveness and semantics, including the documented 64-byte indexed string prefix and lack of indexed stored string-array filtering, ID collisions, pagination, limits, revocation, async sync/deletion, residency, retention and billing. Perform an authoritative current-policy check before exposing any content, citation or side effect. Ensure the provider receives only data it is authorized to process.
- If the provider cannot guarantee pre-ranking isolation for a required access case, select a safely isolated partition or keep that case on a verified alternative; do not fall back to broad retrieval followed by post-filtering. Document residual timing/side-channel limits and use fail-closed behavior on unknown policy, provider outage or freshness.
- Benchmark managed AI Search, the existing Naelao pipeline and an improved independent lexical path using identical authorized corpora and queries. Measure recall/precision, citation correctness, latency, throughput, ingestion/update/delete propagation, maintenance and invoice-level cost. Handle both results and unsupported cases explicitly; never silently switch a tenant or lose canonical data. Generation models and other services are separately billed where applicable.
- For features unavailable in Cloudflare, define a host-controlled adapter and select among verified open-source or third-party services, or implement the missing functionality with bounded Naelao-owned primitives. Assess security isolation, license/model weights, API support, operations, rate limits, region, total cost and exit/migration before adoption. No open-source project is presumed to run inside Workers or to be free to operate.

## 7. No-code dashboard, SDK and extension integration

- Provide a responsive accessible knowledge dashboard: create collections; select Space/project/private scope and audiences; upload/select sources; preview parsed content and citations; configure refresh, retention, language and approved retrieval modes; see cost estimate and status; test questions and compare answers/sources; publish, unpublish, pause, retry, revoke, export and delete. Clearly label unsupported features, draft versus published versions, stale sources and simulated results.
- Offer a versioned SDK and host-mediated extension APIs for declared knowledge requirements, collection discovery, grant requests, status, search, bounded citations and provenance. Extend guided template onboarding and microapp dashboards: a customer-service package can request approved collections, choose channels, test sample questions and review cost/permissions before activation. Agents and extensions receive only explicit effective grants, not raw Cloudflare API keys, cross-project identifiers or direct bucket/index authority.
- Reuse the existing owner-reviewed Project Knowledge selection and signed extension contracts after checking current implementation. Define service-to-service identity and caller permissions for assistants, delegated workers, voice calls and web/WhatsApp/email channels. External channel users do not inherit staff permissions; the host provides only the published subset. A channel or provider integration is advertised only after its own live acceptance.
- Include secure preview/testing with synthetic or authorized data, per-installation settings, localization, mobile access, retryable operations, version migrations and rollback. UI actions call the same authoritative APIs as SDK/agents; a no-code interface cannot bypass billing, permissions, source consent or publication checks.

## 8. Lifecycle, privacy and recovery

- Implement document-level and source-level versioning and deletion as durable, idempotent operations. Tombstones and current ACL checks must fence in-flight ingest, sync, index publication, caches, summaries, external provider mirrors and citations. Propagate revocation promptly; do not promise immediate provider deletion if the contract is asynchronous. Report pending/complete/failed/unknown and reconcile with receipts, retries and operator visibility.
- Define retention, consent, encryption, jurisdiction, legal holds, access logging, exports, backups and provider exit. Preserve currently supported archive/reindex behavior and verify actual coverage: source objects, canonical metadata, ACLs and version references versus derived indexes and external resources. Restore into an isolated environment, reauthorize grants, reindex, verify hashes and support rollback; state actual RPO/RTO only after drills.
- Treat personal and regulated data conservatively; allow collection-level data classification, minimization and policies for customer-facing use. Separate business facts from clinical advice and provide human handoff/escalation for uncertain pharmacy responses; this does not substitute for professional or legal review.

## 9. Cost, limits and observability

- Reuse authoritative Naelao pricing, entitlements, reservations, usage and billing systems. Meter storage, bytes, documents, parsing/OCR, source sync, embedding tokens, index operations, retrieval/rerank/query rewriting, generation, provider calls, background jobs, runtime time and backups by tenant/Space/Project/installation where attributable. Distinguish estimated usage, provider-reported usage, invoices, shared overhead, BYO and managed costs. Do not charge twice for provider-bundled components.
- Provide per-user/project/Space limits, estimates before paid indexing or bulk sync, maximum job cost/size, concurrency/rate controls, alerts and pause of new optional work at budget exhaustion. Preserve authorized existing data and deletion/cleanup flows when a budget expires. Model unknown outcomes, refunds, asynchronous provider charges and reconciliation; never claim a universal provider spend cap.
- As of 15 November 2026 [Cloudflare AI Search pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-search/platform/limits-pricing/) states billing begins 1 December 2026 with account-wide allowances and separately priced ingestion, image processing, storage and queries. Refresh terms at implementation and launch; compare true bills including existing R2, generation and other services, not theoretical unit prices alone. Avoid promises that all users receive the account's allowance individually.
- Record bounded status/metrics: parse failures, jobs and retries, sync lag, index freshness, permission denials, authorization rechecks, deletions, cost, latency, retrieval quality, citation failures and provider outage/recovery. Do not log customer documents, credentials, raw prompts or sensitive private identifiers unnecessarily. Alerts must not leak restricted data.

## 10. Evaluation, rollout and acceptance

Build a consented/synthetic, versioned, human-reviewed benchmark with exact identifiers, paraphrases, multilingual and cross-language questions, tables, scanned files, missing answers, conflicting/old sources, malicious text, multi-document questions and permitted/forbidden access. Measure retrieval recall/precision, citation/source-version correctness, groundedness, answer quality, abstention, latency, freshness and cost. [Ragas metrics](https://docs.ragas.io/en/stable/concepts/metrics/available_metrics/) can inform the approach, but automated judges are fallible; review a representative sample and document model/fixture limitations. Use Zimbabwean language speakers and domain experts for appropriate test sets.

Sequence work without waiting for credentials where independent code is possible:

1. Inventory source-to-UI-to-agent and extension paths, fix the concrete size/scope mismatch, establish benchmark and security baselines, and add per-requirement progress evidence.
2. Complete collections, permissions, durable lifecycle, guided upload and extraction previews; preserve existing compatibility contracts and data.
3. Add secure independently indexed keyword retrieval, fusion and configurable evaluation; benchmark optional reranking and multilingual models with versioned reindex.
4. Add capability-gated rich parsing, website/manual refresh and incremental connector framework; expose safe setup/status and integration SDK. Evaluate managed and alternative backends in isolation rather than assuming selection in advance.
5. Complete customer-service extension selection, no-code test/publish flows, budgets, metering, retention, backup/restore, revocation and operational dashboards.
6. Run deployed, multi-user and real-provider acceptance on disposable scoped resources under explicit cost caps. Roll out narrowly with migration/rollback and monitor quality, safety and spend; keep unsupported services unavailable with clear errors.

Acceptance must include: the same corpus isolated between two tenants and private projects; revoked access at retrieval/citation/cache/channel boundaries; hidden documents not leaking through result counts; all supported file types and accurate table/scan preview with known failures; sync update/delete races, retries and lost responses; semantic misses rescued by authorized lexical search; no-answer and conflicting-source behavior; integration with a signed customer-service extension; budget exhaustion and provider unavailability; backup/restore/reindex and deletion/erasure under concurrency; language and clinical-safety review for advertised workflows; actual deployed evidence distinct from mocks. Do not describe a capability as fully implemented or live accepted while callers, policies, tests or operational work remain open.

## Microapp retrieval through MCP v2

Treat microapps as first-class knowledge and live-data sources using Naelao's existing MCP v2 architecture. Reconcile these requirements against the actual current MCP v2 discovery, authorization, SDK, callers, and knowledge implementation before assigning implementation status; this section is a proposal, not evidence that the full path already works. Reuse existing contracts and requirement IDs where applicable.

- Let extension and template builders declare, through the SDK and a versioned manifest, microapp descriptions, searchable fields and documents, live read tools, permitted actions, input/output schemas, provenance, freshness, scope, and required permissions. Support guided selection and grants by authorized Space or Project administrators; never grant access merely because an app is installed or indexed.
- Discover relevant permitted microapps and MCP v2 tools alongside knowledge collections. Check the requesting employee's current Space and Project membership, installation, role, row/field permissions and grants at discovery and again at retrieval and action time. Prevent private project, record, identifier and count leakage; recheck current authorization before returning indexed or cached results, and invalidate or deny access promptly on revocation.
- Index approved app metadata, descriptions and suitable textual records for permission-aware keyword and semantic discovery. Track source version, deletion and update events, index lag, and rebuilds. Treat the index as a derived, potentially stale projection; preserve the microapp as the authority. Use live, bounded, host-mediated MCP v2 reads for current stock, prices, batches, orders, exact filtering, counts and aggregates. Return timestamps, scope, source identifiers safe for the reader, and completeness or pagination status. If freshness or authority cannot be established, disclose or fail closed as appropriate.
- Support mixed questions with bounded retrieval: e.g. query expiring batch quantities and values from the stock microapp, calculate exact totals in the authorized data layer, then retrieve approved expiry policy passages and provide traceable citations. Do not derive exact totals from partial semantic search results or an LLM summary. Use typed, constrained, parameterized operations rather than giving an agent unrestricted database access. Preserve signed installation boundaries and require existing approvals for mutations.
- Choose simple deterministic retrieval where sufficient; use multi-source planning, parallel searches, reranking and extra model turns only when the task warrants them. Bound calls, returned rows, pagination, token budgets, latency, retries and cost; handle missing tools, stale sources, unknown authorization, unavailable providers and incomplete results explicitly. Treat retrieved data as untrusted instructions.
- Offer optional query-focused extraction or compression after permission filtering and source-side filtering, not as a substitute for exact calculations. Evaluate ScaleDown Extract for specified entity spans and confidence/offsets, and Summarize or Compress for appropriate long text; preserve original evidence and citations, and prohibit silently dropping important qualifiers. Allow approved alternative models or no compression, subject to tenant consent, data handling, jurisdiction and provider policy. Current vendor pages list [Extract through OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/scaledown/extract) and [ScaleDown direct](https://www.scaledown.ai/); prices, availability, retention and terms must be rechecked at admission. Do not assume compression is cheaper for a low-priced answering model.
- Meter and show per-request model calls and tokens, MCP calls, database reads, embeddings, indexing, reranking, extraction/compression, storage, retries and messaging where attributable. Compare the cost and latency of a direct answer, a live tool query, document retrieval, and multi-source reasoning; do not equate an employee message, MCP tool call, model invocation and WhatsApp reply. Use reservations and limits from the existing billing system.
- Evaluate against synthetic and consented pharmacy fixtures and other microapps: current and stale stock, exact batch values, mixed documents and records, conflicting sources, large results, pagination, missing sources, extraction omissions, prompt injection, membership and row/field revocation, tenant isolation and cost ceilings. Compare answers and citations against source records; distinguish mocked, deployed and provider evidence. Validate medication-related outputs with appropriate pharmacy experts and route clinical decisions to authorized professionals.

These inform the design; they do not demonstrate Naelao implementation or live acceptance.
